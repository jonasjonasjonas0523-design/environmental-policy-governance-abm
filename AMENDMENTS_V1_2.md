# AMENDMENTS_V1_2.md
Amendment and freeze record for model version v1.2. Date of freeze content: 2026-08-21. This file, together with the six companion v1.2 files and v1_2_calibration_script.py, constitutes the complete pre-implementation freeze. v1.1 and its entire pre-run package remain archived unchanged; v1.2 files OVERRIDE specific sections of the v1.0/v1.1 package per the override map in Section 6; nothing is edited retroactively.

## 1. Amendment A2 (the only model change besides the reputational construction)
A2 replaces the derived formula for the calibration quantity M_i. v1.1: M_i = one period of the minimized expected cost under the threatened standard. v1.2: M_i(g, t) = m_i(g) x Ann(delta h, T - t), the finite-horizon continuation value of entering the threatened regime at t+1, where m_i(g) is the unchanged one-period closed form and Ann(x, n) = (1 - x^n)/(1 - x), Ann(x, 0) = 0.
Justification, independent of any simulation outcome: (i) internal consistency: the threatened regime is the model's own standard instrument, which by definition applies in every period it is in force; costing it at one period contradicts the model's own object; (ii) the Bellman reading of eq. (6): the single future term delta h V_{t+1} is a continuation value, not a flow; (iii) the interpretation of h most consistent with its existing definition and calibration is per-period regime survival (V1_2_CALIBRATION_MEMO Sec 1), which fixes the (delta h)^r weighting inside Ann. Equations (6) to (13) of the manuscript are unchanged; the A1 exemption structure is unchanged. The one-period convention is retained as a predeclared robustness variant so v1.1 nests inside v1.2's robustness program.
Interpretive note recorded for the manuscript: under A1 + A2 the exemption is a grandfathering safe harbor: verified achievement in the introduction-decision period exempts the firm from the regime introduced at t+1. Grandfathering of early actors is a documented regulatory design; the generosity of this reading is disclosed rather than hidden, and the contemporaneous-exemption alternative is degenerate in this architecture because the voluntary target equals the standard's threshold (achieving it costs weakly more than complying), so it is not adopted.

## 2. Corrections register (errors found and fixed at freeze; none affects the v1.2 decision)
1. DECISION_MEMO_VOLUNTARY_ARM Sec 6.1 wrote the annuity exponent as T - t - 1. Finalized convention: decision at t, introduction at t+1, regime spans t+1..T, so Ann has n = T - t terms. The archived decision memo is not edited; this register is the correction of record. The v1.1 infeasibility proof is unaffected (it uses only nu, the theta ceiling, M/K <= 1, and p, h <= 1; no annuity appears in it).
2. CODE_INPUT_SPECIFICATION Sec 6 and PRE_REGISTRATION Sec 6 item 12 stated tau* "must land slightly above 0.2/e = 0.07358". The uncapped preview neglects the cap min(tau/gamma, E0), which binds for about 17 percent of draws: E[min(0.07358/gamma, E0)] = 0.166, not 0.2. The frozen SOLVE METHOD is unchanged and yields tau* = 0.09339 (reference quadrature, seed 20260821, n = 400,000). The verification expectation is restated in V1_2_CODE_CHECKS Sec 5; f_S = 2 tau* = 0.18678 in normalized units.
3. DECISION_MEMO Sec 6.4 proposed a V5-prime floor of 0.25 x A*_cum bridged from "25 to 50 percent of Dutch industrial energy savings attributable to the agreements". The bridge is indefensible: the empirical share is attribution against an autonomous efficiency trend, and the model contains no autonomous abatement, so the model-side attribution share is 100 percent by construction. The floor is removed; V5-prime is finalized without it (V1_2_VALIDATION_MEMO). Recorded openly: removing the floor weakens one criterion; failability is preserved through the two-component structure and is argued concretely there.
