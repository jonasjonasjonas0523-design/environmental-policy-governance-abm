# Final submission metadata and manuscript-linkage decisions

These items do not alter the frozen model or any computational output.

## Self-supporting manuscript

The final ABM manuscript is self-supporting. The proposed unpublished companion configurational paper was dropped from the submitted manuscript and is not used as empirical warrant for any model claim. Historical development notes that mention a possible companion link are superseded by this decision.

## WGI vintage

Archetype ordering/placement support uses the World Bank Worldwide Governance Indicators **2025 Revision**, with **2024 country observations**, accessed **August 2026**. WGI is used for ordering and contextual placement support only. No WGI score is directly transformed into `p`, `c`, `k`, or `h`.
