### 2.1 V5p-i downgraded to an UNSCORED feasibility and context check

### 2.2 V5p-ii, the scored held-out external voluntary validation test
Statistic: Delta_grad = (cumulative voluntary abatement of the top E0 quintile divided by that quintile's total baseline emissions) minus (the same ratio for the bottom E0 quintile), at the frozen Dutch central placement; quintiles by E0 within each seed's realized population; the intensity normalization prevents a mechanical pass from firm scale.
Empirical anchor: the independent Dutch finding that the agreements were less effective with small enterprises than with large ones (Rietbergen, Farla and Blok 2002), a direction the calibration never touched (the 33/50 tier moment constrains the US placement fit; whether the calibrated mechanism reproduces the size gradient under Dutch governance is not implied by it, per 2.3).
Criterion (locked): 95 percent simultaneous band on Delta_grad strictly above zero (max-t over replicate seeds, B = 2,000; validation seeds principal-disjoint).
Classification: FATAL to external voluntary-instrument claims. With one scored component, its fatality carries the instrument: if the mechanism cannot reproduce the one independent directional pattern held out for it, external voluntary claims fall. One-shot rule applies (Sec 4).

### 2.3 Failability of the single scored test, on its own terms
Delta_grad's sign at the Dutch placement is genuinely open because the two participation channels select opposite tails and the Dutch placement activates both: the reputational channel selects high-E0 firms (thin under the adopted calibration: the 0.0855 core), while the threat channel selects low gamma E0 firms, disproportionately the small and cheap, and p = 0.75 lies far above the ignition threshold 0.2797 with the annuity at its maximum early in the horizon, so threat-driven small-firm participation can be broad while it lasts. Whether the intensity-normalized top-quintile contribution exceeds the bottom-quintile contribution over the full horizon has not been computed anywhere (the freeze contains no Dutch dynamics, gate C5), and the channels' opposition means a negative or null gradient is a live outcome. The eight placement-sensitivity variants (Sec 3) additionally probe whether a central-point pass is knife-edge.
