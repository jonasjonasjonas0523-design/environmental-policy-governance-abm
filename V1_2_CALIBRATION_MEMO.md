## 3. Construct audit of the 33/50 calibration statistic (audit item 2)
Classification of each empirical target:
- "About 15 percent of eligible companies; about 1,294 of more than 8,000 invited": EVER-PARTICIPATED MEMBERSHIP over the program lifetime (a company counted once if it ever committed). Not point-in-time, not annual.
- "About 64 percent of the top first-invitation tier participated (eventually)": EVER-PARTICIPATED MEMBERSHIP for the tier. Same construct.
- "236 of 600 committed by July 1991 (39 percent)": an EARLY POINT-IN-TIME CUMULATIVE SNAPSHOT five months in. A different construct from ever-membership at program scale; it has no clean analogue once the statistic below is adopted, and it is therefore REMOVED from the target and uncertainty sets (AMENDMENTS Sec 2.6). The tier-mapping uncertainty is carried instead by the top-decile variant (Sec 5).
Closest legitimate model analogue: the EVER-participated share over t = 1..T (a firm counts once if d_it = 1 in any period), overall and within the top-7.5-percent-by-E0 tier. The previously frozen time-average annual participation is a DIFFERENT construct: it weights by spell length, equating a one-year participant with one eighth of a permanent participant, which membership counts do not do; it is retired for that reason and not because of any model behavior. Windows: ever over the full t = 1..T (the empirical window is the whole program; an ever-measure is insensitive to the ignition and unraveling endpoints).
Everything else in the calibration procedure is unchanged and was already frozen: targets 0.15 and 0.64; tolerances 0.03 and 0.08; tier = top 7.5 percent by E0 (mirrors about 600 of more than 8,000 invited, ranked by release volume); US placement central (p, c, k, h) = (0.35, 0.85, 0.05, 0.95) with p band [0.20, 0.50]; projection ladder in 0.01 steps, nearest-first; logit least squares over (nu_0, eta) in [0, 3] x [0, 2]; adaptive mean-field path from the model's own initialization; quadrature seed 20260821; screening at 100,000 draws, adoption confirmed at 400,000.

## 4. Solve under the corrected statistic and frozen tau* (full ladder record)
